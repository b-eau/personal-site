Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how beaumitch.com and associated personal software applications, tools, and projects operated by Beau Mitchell ("we", "us", or "our") handle information when you visit this website or use our applications, including applications that integrate with Google OAuth and Google APIs.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate our website and applications:

  • Account & Authentication Data: When you sign in to or authorize one of our applications (such as via Google OAuth), we may receive basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile identifier) and access tokens strictly for the permissions (scopes) you explicitly grant on the consent screen.
  • User-Provided Data: Any data, files, or content you voluntarily submit or configure while using the application.
  • Basic Technical Logs: Standard web hosting logs (such as IP address, browser type, and timestamp) automatically collected by our hosting provider to deliver and secure the service.

2. How We Use Information

Any information accessed or collected is used solely to provide, operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the specific features of the application you choose to use. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Google user data to any third parties, nor do we use it for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

Our applications' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Limited Use: Data obtained through Google APIs is used strictly to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in the requesting application's user interface.
  • No Unauthorized Transfer: We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve the user-facing features you requested, to comply with applicable law, or with your explicit consent.
  • No Advertising or AI/ML Training: We never use Google user data to serve advertisements or to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

We take reasonable technical measures to protect credentials, tokens, and any processed data from unauthorized access or disclosure. Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the requested functionality. Where possible, data is processed ephemerally in memory or stored locally on your own device/account.

5. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You may revoke an application's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page. You may also request deletion of any stored data associated with your account by contacting us at the email address below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact: contact@beaumitch.com.
